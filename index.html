Studio Uploader

A private tool used only by its owner, Sarvarjon Rustamov, to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and to read those channels' statistics.

Privacy Policy

  • It accesses YouTube data only through the owner's own Google account, with the owner's consent.
  • Data it stores (video IDs, titles, view statistics of the owner's channels) stays on the owner's computer and is never shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • It does not collect any data from other users.
  • It uses YouTube API Services: YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
